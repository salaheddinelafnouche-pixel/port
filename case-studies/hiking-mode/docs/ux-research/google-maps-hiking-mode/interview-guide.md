# Interview guide: Google Maps Hiking Mode

Status: Synthesized case study instrument (not conducted with real participants)

## Format
45–60 minutes remote or in-person; semi-structured; record with consent; anonymise.

## Focus
Tool abandonment on rugged mountain terrain, day-commit rituals, and dirt trust vs road trust.

## Participants
8 total: 6 serious backpacker / long-distance; 2 day/weekend.

## Script

### Intro (3 min)
Explain purpose: understand how hikers plan and navigate multi-day travel and where Google Maps helps or fails — especially once terrain turns rugged. Consent. No right answers.

### Warm-up (W1–W3)

**W1.** How would you describe yourself as a hiker, tracker, or outdoor athlete?
- Probes: Day trips, weekends, multi-day sections, or long-distance? What terrain do you seek most often? Alone, with a partner, or in a small group?

**W2.** Walk me through your most recent overnight or longer trip in a few sentences.
- Probes: Where did you start and end? What went smoothly, and what stressed you? How prepared did you feel the night before you left?

**W3.** When do you open Google Maps for outdoor trips today?
- Probes: Roads and parking only, or also on dirt? Towns, lodging, or resupply? What do you never use Maps for?

### Theme 1 — Tool sequence & abandonment (RQ1) · Q1–Q3

**Q1.** Walk me through your last multi-day (or longest) hike from planning at home to arriving in town — which apps at which moments?
- Probes: Where does Google Maps show up first? When do specialty apps take over? What triggers a switch mid-day?

**Q2.** Tell me about a moment on rugged mountain terrain when you stopped trusting Google Maps and switched away.
- Probes: What did the map show versus what you saw on the ground? Junction, grade, trail line, or signal? How did you decide which other app to open?

**Q3.** What would have to be true for you to stay in Maps past the gravel lot?
- Probes: Trail continuity? Offline? Elevation? Bailouts? What would still make you keep a backup app? Have you ever tried staying in Maps longer than usual?

### Theme 2 — Failure stories on rugged terrain (RQ2) · Q4–Q5

**Q4.** Tell me about a time navigation or day planning went wrong in steep, remote, or unsigned terrain.
- Probes: Wrong fork, elevation surprise, dry water, offline gap, or no bailout? What information were you missing? How did you recover — and what did it cost you?

**Q5.** Which on-trail failure do you fear most when the mountains get rugged — and why that one?
- Probes: How often has it happened versus how often you prepare for it? Does fear change packing or downloads? Who do you tell when something goes wrong?

### Theme 3 — Committing to the next day (RQ3) · Q6–Q7

**Q6.** How do you decide tomorrow’s miles the evening before a hard mountain day?
- Probes: What must you see before you sleep? What do you ignore even if apps show it? Where do distance, gain, water, camp, and exits live today?

**Q7.** Describe your “day-commit” ritual — screenshots, notes, paper, or talking it through.
- Probes: How many apps or screens does it take? What breaks when battery or signal is low? What would a single honest sheet need to include?

### Theme 4 — Trust on pavement vs dirt (RQ4) · Q8–Q9

**Q8.** When do you trust Google Maps completely on an outdoor trip?
- Probes: Driving, parking, walking into town? Any dirt moments where it earned trust? What language do you use when recommending Maps to other hikers?

**Q9.** When would you refuse to rely on Maps for dirt navigation — even with signal?
- Probes: Missing trail emphasis, grade, or offline confidence? Past bad experience versus general reputation? What would change that refusal?

### Theme 5 — Consolidation & lighter needs (RQ5 · RQ6) · Q10–Q12

**Q10.** If a Hiking mode lived inside Google Maps, what would it need on day one for a multi-day mountain section?
- Probes: Must-haves versus nice-to-haves? What would feel like “too much” or “too casual”? Would offline corridor + bailouts be deal-breakers?

**Q11.** For day or weekend hikers you know — what do they need that serious backpackers might not?
- Probes: Difficulty and time versus multi-day resupply? What would overwhelm them? Would a lighter preset keep them in Maps?

**Q12.** How likely would you be to make Maps your primary on-trail map if Hiking mode covered continuity, elevation, offline pack, and exits?
- Probes: What would still keep a specialty app installed? After one successful offline corridor, would habit change? What proof would you need before trusting it on a remote stretch?

### Close
Is there anything we did not ask that matters for staying found and on plan when the trail gets steep, remote, or unsigned?

## Note-taking
Capture verbatim phrases for Says; tag inferences as Hypothesis until triangulated with survey and social listening.
