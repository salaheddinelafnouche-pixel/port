# Journey map: Maya Chen

Status: Provisional hypothesis (synthesized research)

## Scenario
Maya plans a four-day mountain section between resupply towns — Maps for the drive, then abandoned at the lot.

## Stages

Short labels (emotion curve): Plan · Approach · On trail · Camp · Remote · Town

### 1. Pre-trip planning (home / hostel Wi‑Fi)
- Actions: Sketches daily miles, water, camps across apps; downloads offline packs.
- Thoughts: Can I make town by day four without a dry camp?
- Emotion: hopeful
- Pain points: Scattered data; no continuous trail-first day plan.
- Opportunities: Unify corridor planning with water, camp, and bailouts.

### 2. Approach to trailhead (driving)
- Actions: Drives in Maps to parking; locks offline before coverage drops.
- Thoughts: Once I leave pavement, does my trail plan still work?
- Emotion: neutral
- Pain points: Handoff anxiety from Maps roads to specialty trail apps.
- Opportunities: Mode switch from driving to hiking without losing context.

### 3. First miles on trail
- Actions: Confirms junctions; glances at elevation for the climb ahead.
- Thoughts: Is this the right fork under a full pack?
- Emotion: anxious
- Pain points: Weak footpath continuity and grade; wrong-fork fear.
- Opportunities: Readable footpaths and grade at junctions and climbs.

### 4. Evening camp / next-day commit
- Actions: Reviews miles and water; adjusts day length; shares location.
- Thoughts: Tomorrow must be honest about gain and water.
- Emotion: frustrated
- Pain points: No day-commit sheet; battery burned switching apps.
- Opportunities: Day plan with distance, elevation, water, camp, share.

### 5. Remote stretch (low or no signal)
- Actions: Uses offline map; rations battery; watches for bailout spurs.
- Thoughts: If I need out, where is the nearest road?
- Emotion: anxious
- Pain points: Offline gaps; unclear exits; advisories not on the corridor.
- Opportunities: Offline corridor with bailouts visible without signal.

### 6. Resupply town arrival
- Actions: Uses Maps for motel, food, laundry; plans the next dirt section.
- Thoughts: Same map for town chores and the next section.
- Emotion: relieved
- Pain points: Rebuilding trail context after town days in another app.
- Opportunities: Keep hiking corridor and town nav in one Maps session.
