# Affinity clusters: Google Maps Hiking Mode

Status: Provisional hypothesis (UCD affinity mapping of interview notes, survey metrics, and social codes)

## C1 — Road layer vs dirt layer
- Insight: Maps is the default for pavement, parking, and town; specialty apps own footpath continuity once the lot ends.
- Evidence: Hypothesis · F1 · 79% dual-app same trip; social “switch after parking” codes
- Design implication: Hiking mode must bridge the gravel-lot handoff without a new brand.

## C2 — Continuity and exits over discovery
- Insight: Wrong junctions, offline gaps, dry water, and missing bailouts outrank “find a scenic trail” as stress drivers.
- Evidence: Hypothesis · F2 · top stress rates 61% / 54% / 49% / 47%
- Design implication: Prioritize trail continuity and exits over gallery-style discovery.

## C3 — Honest next-day sheet
- Insight: Backpackers need distance, gain, water, camp, and bailout in one place the night before a mountain day.
- Evidence: Hypothesis · F3 · must-have rates 92% / 88% / 85% / 71% / 69%
- Design implication: Day-commit clarity is a primary job, not a secondary tip card.

## C4 — Dirt-trust gap
- Insight: Maps earns high road trust and low dirt trust; the gap is trail emphasis, grade, and offline confidence — not chrome dislike.
- Evidence: Hypothesis · F4 · trust 4.6 roads vs 2.4 dirt vs 4.1 specialty dirt
- Design implication: Earn dirt trust through readable trails, grade, and offline-first corridors.

## C5 — Subset, not a fork
- Insight: Day/weekend hikers share junction clarity and difficulty needs but reject multi-day density.
- Evidence: Hypothesis · F5 · 72% want simpler Hiking mode vs road-only
- Design implication: One mode with a lighter preset — not a separate product.

## C6 — Willingness to consolidate
- Insight: Backpackers will consider Maps primary on trail if multi-day plan, elevation, offline pack, and bailouts ship together.
- Evidence: Hypothesis · F6 · 68% intent 4–5; interviews condition on signal-free proof
- Design implication: Ship backpacker-led depth first; prove offline before claiming dirt trust.
